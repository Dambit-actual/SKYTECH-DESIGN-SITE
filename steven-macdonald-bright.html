01 — Profile

Who I Am

I'm a commercial drone pilot and UAS specialist with over 15 years of operational experience flying and building multirotor and fixed-wing platforms. I hold a current Australian Remote Pilot Licence (RePL) with sub-25 kg endorsement and have operated professionally across Southeast Queensland and the United Arab Emirates.

My background spans aerial inspection, flight planning, airspace coordination, and operational risk assessment — paired with a deep technical foundation in UAV design, custom fabrication, and flight control systems including Betaflight, ArduPilot, and PX4.

I'm not just a pilot. I understand the aircraft from the frame up. That means I can diagnose issues in the field, maintain platforms independently, and contribute to a team's technical capability — not just its headcount.
